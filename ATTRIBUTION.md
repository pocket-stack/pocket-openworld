# Asset and research record

## Environment and original content

Environment geometry and small color textures are generated at runtime by
`src/art.rs` from mathematical primitives and fixed seeds.

The explorer model, rig, materials, axe, and animation are original work
generated by `assets/character/generate_character.py`. The editable Blender
source, runtime GLB, validation receipt, and studio previews are generated from
that checked-in script. They are covered by this repository's MIT license.

## Local Frieren character

The active local character is based on the free `frieren (.fbx .blend)` model
by dedastore, downloaded by the repository owner from
<https://booth.pm/ja/items/5469071>. The supplied Blender source and texture
are not original Pocket Openworld work and are not covered by this repository's
MIT license.

`assets/character/import_frieren.py` creates new `Idle`, `Walk`, `Chop`,
`Cast`, and `Water` actions on the supplied rig, normalizes its material to a
glTF PBR base-color texture, and exports the local runtime GLB and QA previews.
The BOOTH page describes the download as free but does not provide an explicit
redistribution license. The generated model bytes are confined to this private
repository and must not be copied into a public repository, release, package,
or other redistribution channel without separate permission and a copyright
review.

## World Law character models

The World Law POC includes an adapted **EVANGELION UNIT ONE** model by
**BROWNCOAT** and an adapted **Colossal Titan** model by **Sidaivan**. Their
source pages designate both models CC BY 4.0:

- <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/evangelion-unit-one-07081cd3a70e494095271c43a591af81>
- <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/colossal-titan-e031a57fd4bf411f8e893361676b4544>

The EVA source description additionally states `creds` for
<https://byneet.fanbox.cc/>; that supplied credit is preserved in
`assets/world-law/model-receipt.json`.

`assets/world-law/import_models.py` changes scale and origin, removes viewer
helpers, poses and bakes the Titan rig, and splits its screen-right arm by skin
weights for the fracture/regeneration presentation. The derived GLBs remain
CC BY 4.0 and are not covered by the surrounding MIT code license. Exact
source hashes, Objaverse archive paths, output hashes, and geometry bounds are
recorded in `assets/world-law/model-receipt.json`.

Evangelion and Attack on Titan character designs, names, and associated marks
remain the property of their respective rights holders. The source model
licenses do not grant rights beyond the contributors' work.

## Research boundary

The public [zeldaret/botw](https://github.com/zeldaret/botw) project was
consulted to understand observed high-level relationships between actors,
physics contacts, physical materials, reactive materials, world conditions,
tree damage, and attached items. That repository did not provide a reuse
license at the researched revision.

No source, names, type layouts, constants, equations, configuration schemas,
shaders, extracted data, or assets from that project or from Nintendo software
are included here. Runtime types, algorithms, numeric tuning, art, controls,
and test scenarios were designed independently for PocketJS.
